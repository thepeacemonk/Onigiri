Welcome to Onigiri

Onigiri (お握り) is a comprehensive Anki add-on designed to completely overhaul the user interface and user experience of Anki. Our philosophy is built on three pillars:

  • Anki is Democratic: Everyone deserves a beautiful, personalized experience.
  • Design Cultivates Motivation: A calm, cohesive interface lowers friction and encourages daily study habits.
  • Calm Defaults, Powerful Options: The base experience is clean, but beneath the surface lies immense customization.

How to Navigate

All of Onigiri's capabilities can be customized via the Onigiri Settings accessible through the main Anki menubar under Onigiri > Onigiri Settings. In the settings, you'll find various tabs that correspond to the sections in this Wiki.

Modes

Onigiri allows you to alter the fundamental layout of Anki to suit your workflow. You can change these in the General tab of the settings.

Available Modes

  • Full: The standard Anki layout with all native toolbars and menus intact, but skinned with Onigiri's colors.
  • Flow: Hides the native Anki top menu bar (File, Edit, View, etc.) on Windows/Linux to provide a cleaner app window. Note: This specifically affects Windows and Linux.
  • Focus: Replaces the native Anki top bar (Decks, Add, Browse, Stats, Sync) with Onigiri's sleek, modern top bar. Includes everything from Flow mode.
  • Zen: The ultimate distraction-free mode. Hides the Reviewer bottom bar (answer buttons only navigation) and the Onigiri top bar. Includes everything from Focus mode.

Profile Settings

Personalize your study dashboard by configuring your profile. Found in the Profile tab.

User Details

  • User Name: Set the name that appears at the top of the Main Menu and in your Profile Card.
  • Birthday: Set your birthday. Onigiri might have a surprise for you!
  • Profile Picture: Upload a custom avatar (PNG or JPG). It will appear in the top right corner and on your profile screen.

Profile Page Styling

You can customize the appearance of the Profile screen specifically:

  • Profile Backgrounds: Set a specific image or solid color for the Profile screen background.
  • Section Visibility: Choose which elements (Stats, User Details) appear when you open your profile.

Main Menu & Widgets

The dashboard is the heart of Onigiri. You can fully customize what information is shown to you in the Main Menu tab.

Organize Grid

Onigiri uses a Widget Grid system to display your stats. You can drag and drop widgets to reorder them, move them to the archive to hide them, and change the number of columns in the grid.

Available Widgets

  • Studied Card: Shows how many cards you've reviewed today.
  • Time Card: Shows your total study time for the day.
  • Pace Card: Shows your average speed (cards per minute/hour).
  • Retention Card: Displays your retention rate for the day. You can customize the "Star Icon" for this widget.
  • Heatmap: A visual representation of your study streaks. You can change the color of the active days and the zero-review days independently of the main theme.
  • Favorites Widget: A quick-access list of your most studied or important decks.
  • Restaurant Level: Shows your current Gamification level and XP progress.

Sidebar & Decks

Customize the deck list (the main tree view of your Anki decks) under the Sidebar tab.

Deck Icons

Onigiri intelligently differentiates between types of decks:

  • Folder Icon: Decks that contain subdecks inside them.
  • Deck Icon: Top-level decks with no subdecks.
  • Subdeck Icon: Decks nested inside folders.
  • Filtered Deck Icon: Custom study or filtered decks.

You can hide specific icons, or upload custom SVG/PNG images to replace the default icons globally or per-deck.

Customization Options

  • Deck Indentation: Adjust how far subdecks are indented from their parent folders.
  • Counts: Choose to hide all card counts, or only hide "Zero Counts" (decks with 0 due cards) for a cleaner look.
  • Action Buttons: Organize the visibility and order of deck action buttons (the gear icon, expand/collapse arrows).

Study Pages

Control the appearance of the Overview (deck summary before studying) and the Reviewer (where you answer cards) under the Study Pages tab.

Overviewer

  • Overview Style: Choose between "Pro Overview" (detailed, large stats) or "Mini Overview" (compact, takes up less space).
  • Overview Background: By default, it uses the Main Background, but you can set a specific image or color just for the Overview screen, along with custom blur and opacity.

Reviewer

  • Answer Buttons: Fully customize the Again, Hard, Good, and Easy buttons. Change their border radius, padding, height, and colors (background and text for both normal and hover states).
  • Reviewer Background: Set a custom image or color behind your flashcards. You can match it to the main background or keep it distinct.
  • Bottom Bar: Customize the background color and opacity of the bottom bar where the answer buttons sit.
  • Notification Position: Choose where pop-up notifications (like Gamification updates or sync statuses) appear during reviews.
  • Congrats Screen: Choose whether to show the Profile Bar on the screen that appears when you finish a deck.

Themes & Colors

Onigiri shines in its aesthetic flexibility. Manage this in the Themes and Palette tabs.

Official Themes

Choose from a variety of built-in palettes inspired by popular coding themes, such as: Catppuccin (Latte, Frappé, Macchiato, Mocha), Dracula, Nord, Rose Pine, Tokyo Drift, and more.

Customizing the Palette

You can override any specific color within a theme. Colors are separated into Light Mode and Dark Mode. You can change:

  • Accent Color: The main brand color for buttons and highlights.
  • Background & Foreground: Main canvas colors and text colors.
  • Canvas Inset & Borders: Colors for cards and separator lines.
  • Glassmorphism (Frosted Effect): Adjust the intensity of the blur effect on overlays and UI elements.
  • Shadows & Overlays: Adjust the darkness of shadows and semi-transparent modal backgrounds.

Background Images

In the Gallery, you can upload `.png` images and apply them to various parts of the app (Main Menu, Sidebar, Reviewer). You can set different images for Light and Dark modes, and apply blur filters.

Gamification

Turn studying into a rewarding game. Configure this in the Gamification tab.

Restaurant Level

As you study cards, you earn XP. Earn enough XP to level up your Restaurant! You start as a humble "Onigiri Stand" and can evolve into an "Onigiri Cart", a "Restaurant", and eventually a "Legendary Restaurant". The progress bar is visible on the Main Menu.

Taiyaki Coins & Store

Every review grants you a chance to earn Taiyaki Coins. You can access Mr. Taiyaki's Store from the Onigiri menu. Here, you can spend your coins to unlock Premium Restaurant Themes (like Astronigiri, Lunar New Year, or Coffee & Co.) which change the look of your Restaurant widget.

Study Companions

  • Motivated Mochi: A cute mochi companion that pops up periodically during your reviews to cheer you on with messages like "You got this!" or "Almost there!". You can configure how often Mochi appears.
  • Focus Dango: Write your own custom encouraging messages (one per line in the settings). Focus Dango will randomly display these messages during reviews to keep you motivated.

Focused Gaming

If you find notifications distracting, enable "Focused Gaming" to suppress level-up and coin notifications while you are actively reviewing cards.
